# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume positive intent.
- Offer feedback focused on code and ideas.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or hateful conduct
- Personal attacks or intimidation
- Sharing private information without consent

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, or contributions that violate this code of conduct and may take additional moderation actions when necessary.
